We know the journey

Starting in a new market is hard.

CatchDog is a Miami-based startup founded by people from Latin America. We understand how difficult it can be to find the right resources, build a useful network and get early capital when you are entering a new ecosystem.

Our role is to help founders focus their story, understand the market and approach the right opportunities with clear materials and realistic expectations.

Practical founder support

Get ready for the conversations that matter.

Hands-on work that helps investors understand what you are building, why it matters and why your team can deliver it.

01

Sharpen your business story

Turn a complex idea into a simple, credible explanation of the problem, solution, business model and reason to act now.

02

Build an investor-ready deck

Create a useful pitch deck with a clear structure, relevant evidence and the information early-stage investors expect to see.

03

Understand your market

Research your industry, competitors, customers and market dynamics so your strategy is grounded in facts rather than broad claims.

04

Find realistic opportunities

Identify relevant investors, programs and introductions based on your sector, stage, geography and actual fundraising needs.

05

Present with clarity

Practice the pitch, prepare for difficult questions and communicate your company with confidence in meetings and follow-ups.

06

Plan the next steps

Leave with a practical fundraising plan: who to contact, what to improve and which milestones can make the next conversation stronger.

A focused process

From raw idea to a credible investor conversation.

STEP 02

STEP 03

Early support

Sometimes the first check matters most.

For a small number of startups that strongly match our interests, stage and values, CatchDog may consider a small first check to help the team begin its journey.

Advisory does not guarantee investment or introductions. Every opportunity is reviewed separately, and founders keep a clear view of what is realistic at each stage.
