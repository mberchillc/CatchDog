CatchDog project / SmileAccess

Dental care should not fail at financing.

SmileAccess bridges dental health and financial inclusion for immigrant patients with thin or invisible credit histories.

01 / Problem

The Double Pain Crisis

When a patient is already in physical pain, the financing process adds a second pain: repetitive forms, credit rejection and humiliation at the exact moment they need care.

20-minute barrierPatients repeat the same information across multiple financing applications.
Thin-file rejectionTraditional lenders often miss real ability to pay when immigrants lack standard credit history.
Walkout riskCases become ghost budgets: diagnosed treatments that never turn into care.
02 / Solution

One intake. Multiple paths.

SmileAccess turns the financing search into a structured decision engine: one form, parallel routing, instant options and fallback layers when traditional financing says no.

03 / Zero-Uncertainty Workflow

Four layers before the patient walks out.

The system is designed as a fallback architecture. Each failed layer automatically triggers the next best path.

1
Multi-lender sprintParallel submissions to specialized dental-financing partners.
2
Market expansionBroader personal-loan search for a reasonable chance of approval.
3
Collaborative matchNearby affiliated clinics can accept a case through an idle-chair protocol.
4
Mutual fund interventionA community-backed fund can distribute risk when no clinic can absorb it alone.

05 / Business model

SaaS plus fintech infrastructure.

Clinics subscribe to the automation engine. Premium modules can manage in-house funding, contracts, collections and future API integrations with dental practice management software.

Subscription SaaSMonthly access to the financing workflow and clinic-facing decision tools.
Premium funding moduleAutomated in-house funding management, contracts and collections.
Future integrationsAPIs with dental practice management software and financing partners.
06 / Go-to-market

The Miami 20 launch.

The first GTM wedge is an invite-only founder circle of 20 high-volume dental clinics in Miami, using direct industry relationships and real operational pain as the entry point.

20
clinics in the initial South Florida launch circle.

The goal is precision entry: validate with clinics that already see high-value treatment cases and financing drop-off.

07 / Validation

Born inside a real clinic context.

The concept was modeled from Upper Dental & Aesthetics in Miami, including ghost-budget analysis, high-value treatments and referral patterns between clinics with different occupancy levels.
